## Implemented behavior

The standalone profile `twowiki-semantic-quality/v3-development` uses:

- `semantic_quality_v3.py`: validator `question-bound-relation-grammar/v3-development`, prompt `relation-specific-sentence/v3-development`, retry `relation-specific-bounded-revision/v3-development`.
- `semantic_quality_targets_v3.py`: evidence/review-gated target method, with explicit abstention and deterministic pre-generation selection.
- `semantic_quality_profile_v3.py`: development-only descriptor, source/configuration pins and all-or-none construction API.
- `configs/twowiki_semantic_quality_v3_development.json`: fixed 150-token/two-attempt budgets, greedy decoding, seed 42, intensities 1/3 and duplicate policy.

The new validator is a finite grammar, not a relaxed call to the old validator. A statement must fully match a complete question-bound form, with exact names, title qualifiers, roles, polarity, prepositions and comparison direction. Generic full-answer equality/calendar alias exclusions, prompt leakage and length checks remain explicit. All unmatched material, disclaimers, uncertainty, added facts and extra assertions fail closed. No substring-based target support is accepted. Legacy negation/gold screens remain unchanged for legacy profiles; in the new grammar their scope is resolved structurally rather than deleted globally.

Boolean negative answers can assert the negative relation without denying the target. Titles such as **Yes, Giorgio** and **Jack Reacher: Never Go Back** must remain complete. Comparisons permit the gold option only in its exact operand role and must choose the non-gold option with the requested birth-order direction. A target that is older when asked for younger fails. Kinship forms preserve the exact requested family relationship and complete owner. Date forms use `on`; place forms use `in` or bounded place-of-birth/death paraphrases; nationality uses `is/was`. The film-director death-place relative clause seen in the audit is supported without making the film a person.

Prompts expose the relation-specific canonical sentence form and instruct preservation of roles and directions. Retries repeat this form with the failure reason, under the same token budget and target. This is intentionally constrained sentence generation, close to a template-copy task. A higher future heuristic yield would not establish open-ended generation quality or semantic validity. The template is an instruction, never substituted into the model's output or silently accepted when generation fails. Real-model adherence remains untested.

The construction API requires a decoding configuration attestation and explicit backend-seeding callback, derives a stable seed per query/slot and records raw output, logical/backend prompts, prompt hash, versions, model provenance, timing/token metadata, attempt outcome/reason, validator assessment, seed and profile hash. Infrastructure exceptions propagate with available attempt records attached; callers must archive them instead of relabeling them as semantic failure. Every declared slot is attempted. Exhausted slots cause an all-or-none failure with successful partials archived separately and no injectible documents. Exact/punctuation-only duplicates are retained and reported, never described as independent corroboration. Generation cannot silently expand retries or switch targets.

`construct` is a standalone callable returning serializable records, not a modification to the frozen experiment runner. In a separately authorized pilot, the caller must persist its output atomically in a fresh namespace and seed the backend through the required callback. No filesystem writer, QA evaluation adapter, model loader or new CLI that might accidentally execute a frozen run was added.

## Remaining limits

Only documented question forms and finite paraphrases are supported. Ambiguous/multiple conjunctions, general reasoning, cause-of-death, arbitrary relational composition, unlisted family roles, incomplete dates, unlisted languages and unconstrained paraphrases abstain or reject. The 40-character floor remains and can reject short but otherwise sensible sentences. Optional articles and explicit comparison punctuation variants are bounded; title qualifiers are never stripped. The validator cannot verify factual truth, source quality, type identity, chronology or genealogy by itself. Review metadata cannot authenticate a human or prove independence. Geography and aliases require explicit evidence rather than automatic inference. Sparse evidence can therefore keep coverage low.

The new profile is deliberately absent from frozen registries, preventing historical protocols from selecting it accidentally. A fresh pilot must pin the new descriptor, source, cohort, exposure registry, corpus, model, reviewed target packets and protocol before inference. Its construction, semantic review and eventual QA outcomes must remain separate. The prospective draft specifies these gates and retains historical metric definitions if a later QA stage is separately authorized. Existing two-query Dense/RCD differences provide no general defense-effectiveness evidence.
