# Target selection revision audit — 2026-11-04

Method: `twowiki-evidence-gated-targets/v3-development`, implemented in `src/attacks/semantic_quality_targets_v3.py`. Baseline: `07499fd7d3dc750d9ec876465c76c4972a9c70df`. The frozen selector and its outcomes are unchanged. This method filters explicitly supplied candidates; it does not mine new targets, infer NER, or force alternatives for historical failures.

| Audited problem | New development rule | Abstention evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Person-shaped but incompatible family member | Exact requested role and referent binding; manually evidenced compatible roles; subject and target birth/death dates; chronological screen; human review | Missing facts/citations, incompatible role, reversed birth chronology, implausible age gap |
| Sachar death in 1759 before father's 1899 birth | Full calendar dates; death cannot precede documented birth | `death_precedes_documented_birth` |
| Equivalent dates/aliases | Normalized gold/alias exclusion plus calendar equivalence in admitted full English date formats; reviewed complete alias list bound to query | `gold_or_alias_equivalent_target`, `alias_or_corpus_gold_binding_mismatch` |
| America used against a city answer | Cited geographic granularity for gold and target must match (city/county/region/country) | `geographic_granularity_mismatch` |
| Film/song substituted for person | Relation kind, complete question referent, referent type and target type are checked; grammar preserves the director/performer role | `role_or_subject_mismatch`, `incompatible_referent_type`, `incompatible_target_type` |
| Non-gold label mistaken for independently false target | All gold-support IDs cited, pinned document bytes and exact spans; evidence annotations for corpus gold, identity and type; human contradiction/plausibility review | `gold_support_not_cited`, `untrusted_source_bytes`, missing review/checks |
| Six original nomination abstentions | Date-of-birth/death and birthplace question forms may now be described by grammar, but no automatic new nominees; cause-of-death remains unsupported | `no_independently_supported_candidate` or unsupported relation |

Plausibility bounds are new development policy, not biological laws: inferred lifespan from a proposed birth/death date is limited to 0–120 years; parent/in-law birth gaps are 12–100 years and grandparent gaps 12–120. These necessary checks cannot establish kinship, sex/gender compatibility, or genealogical plausibility. The reviewer must assess these explicitly using cited evidence. Kinship candidates require the requested role in `compatible_roles`, with every required fact linked to a cited span. Unknown birth/death evidence abstains, including otherwise plausible candidates. This intentionally prioritizes evidence over coverage.

Packet contract: `payload` contains `query_sha256=digest(query)`, exact `target`, `nominator_id`, `evidence` (doc ID, exact start/end/quote), `facts`, and `fact_citations` (fact key to evidence-index list). Required common facts are `relation_kind`, `referent`, `referent_type`, `target_type`, `gold_aliases` (gold plus explicitly supplied aliases), and `corpus_gold_answer`. Place relations additionally need both granularities; date relations need referent life dates; kinship needs compatible roles and target/referent life dates. See the synthetic packet factory in the regression tests for the exact shape; those packets are unit fixtures, not actual approvals.

`review` binds the whole payload SHA-256, human reviewer ID distinct from nominator, timezone-aware timestamp, approval decision, strict Boolean checks, and substantive notes. All six checks are required: referent identity, type/role, aliases, corpus-relative contradiction, chronology, and geographic granularity (explicitly reviewed as inapplicable where appropriate). A rejected/uncertain/missing review never selects a target. Selection is deterministic by candidate digest with seed 42, before generation, independent of candidate input order. No target replacement follows a generation failure.

Document pins must come from a separately trusted manifest, not be computed from untrusted input and called independent evidence. Byte/span checks bind citations but cannot prove that an annotation follows from a span. Review records attest manual interpretation; the code does not authenticate reviewer identity or actual independence, and it does not establish global factual truth. No real candidates were newly approved in this task. Candidate evidence and human review for a fresh pilot remain required inputs.
