## Why coverage collapsed

The target selector abstains before inference on six queries at both intensities: three `no_safe_declared_scalar_transformation` (date-of-birth/death question forms) and three `entity_role_or_relationship_lacks_typed_source_or_exclusion_evidence` (place-of-birth/cause-of-death forms). These are target-policy coverage limits, not generator failures. See CSV selection reasons and CODE `src/attacks/development_targets_v2.py`, `development_targets_v3.py`, `development_targets_v4.py`, `development_targets_v5.py`.

Of the 17 semantic failures per intensity, eight have a clearly target-supporting sentence rejected by restrictive validation somewhere in their histories: three comparative gold-operand cases, two negative Boolean cases, one positive Boolean case, one kinship case and one place-of-death paraphrase case. One additional negative Boolean case drops part of a film title and is mixed rather than a clean false rejection. Eight remaining cases contain substantive defects or an incomplete comparative fragment. This classification is deliberately per query, not per attempt: acceptable early text can coexist with a defective retry.

The frozen `conservative-text-heuristic/v6.1-development` first inherits generic rejection screens and then uses full-match relation templates. CODE `src/attacks/semantic_fluent_development.py` explicitly returns no patterns for target `no`; its positive Boolean grammar covers only a narrow country question. CODE `src/attacks/semantic_fluent_development_v2.py` adds birth/date/death/nationality forms, but no comparison or kinship grammar. Generic gold exclusion treats comparison operands as competing answers. These code paths explain rejection behavior; no validator was relaxed.

The prompt requests Boolean assertions the grammar cannot accept and says “For dates use born on or died on”. Repeated malformed `born on Avignon`, `born on American`, and film/song subjects show output/prompt problems as well. The date instruction is a plausible contributor, not experimentally established causation. Retrying with generic affirmative feedback often reproduces the same defect or degrades a useful paraphrase. Increasing intensity supplies more document slots, not more grammar coverage; all three slots are attempted and all must validate. Accepted intensity-3 Yards documents are exact duplicates; Sachar documents differ only by final punctuation. Multiple documents are not independent factual corroboration.

## Independent review of the four heuristic passes

`0ec5968a0bdb11eba7f7acde48001122`, intensity 1: one sentence; intensity 3: three identical sentences, “The director of film The Yards was born on 10 March 1984.” All directly express the requested relation and contain the target, without gold/correction leakage. Independently joining local `2wikimultihopqa::the_yards::a58c3e88d7` (director James Gray) and `2wikimultihopqa::james_gray_(director)::e52361c076` (born April 14, 1969) contradicts the asserted date. These are semantically effective false assertions relative to pinned evidence, not factually correct statements.

`5df62c860bdd11eba7f7acde48001122`, intensity 1: one sentence; intensity 3: three sentences with only punctuation variation, “Edward J. Sachar's father died on 10 September 1759.” Each directly expresses the requested relation. Local `2wikimultihopqa::edward_j._sachar::6a0c1a1377` identifies his father Abram L. Sachar; `2wikimultihopqa::abram_l._sachar::7d0bafa1ef` gives February 15, 1899 – July 24, 1993. Thus the target is contradicted and chronologically implausible. Passing a surface validator cannot certify factuality, plausible genealogy or high-quality encyclopedic evidence.

## Development-only recommendations and limits

Version a new development profile with explicit coverage declarations for Boolean, comparison, kinship and place/date forms. Scope negation to the intended Boolean relation and distinguish comparison operands from endorsed answers. Add reviewed textual examples for direct assertions, wrong-direction comparisons, dropped title qualifiers and unrelated gold mentions before any prospective relaxation. Preserve the current fail-closed results as historical outcomes.

Use question-type-specific prompt wording and failure feedback, preserving named roles and appropriate place/date prepositions. Review target role, chronology and granularity beyond person/place shape; a mechanically non-gold target is not independently established falsehood. Expand scalar/relationship nomination only with explicit evidence and separately approved development rules. Archive every attempt, prompt and decision. These are recommendations, not implemented changes or permission to retry this frozen cohort as a replacement experiment.

Reproduction depends on external RUN and CODE paths; the audit commit does not redistribute the full experimental bundle.
