## New v3 implementation
- `semantic_target_constraints_v3`: date equivalence and chronological bounds.
- `semantic_evidence_binding_v3`: pinned source-document relationship checks.
- `development_targets_v6`: evidence-aware nomination and review-only queue.
- `semantic_target_review_v3`: query/answer/source-hash-bound human-review gate.
- No real target approvals have been generated by these tests.
- The review gate validates review records; it cannot cryptographically
  establish that a human genuinely performed the review.

## Important limitations
- No v3 semantic-generation grammar or document-diversity policy yet.

- Generated poison passages still require their own independent review.
- No new model calls, retrieval evaluation, or held-out runs were made.
- Raw datasets, individual questions, and model transcripts are excluded.

## Two-AI provisional review policy
- Added `semantic_target_review_ai_v3.py`, development-only.
- Requires two provenance-bound AI assessments from distinct model IDs.
- Checks query, answer, supporting-source hashes and explicit decisions.
- Rejects disagreement, uncertainty, missing checks and unresolved relations.
- First AI shortlisted 18; second AI provisionally accepted 16 and
  downgraded 2 to uncertain.
- The 16 are **not automatically construction-approved**.
- AI reviewer identities and response hashes are self-reported metadata,
  not authenticated evidence of independence or correctness.
- The AI policy is not yet connected to the v3 selector or runner.
- Existing human-review implementation is retained for historical
  compatibility until the next explicit migration.

## Explicit AI selector integration
- Added `nominate_ai` as a development-only, opt-in v3 entrypoint.
- Both AI assessments must pass the policy checks.
- Missing, uncertain or conflicting reviews block construction eligibility.
- AI screening never sets factual approval or injection approval.
- Historical human-review code remains untouched.
- Synthetic tests do not approve any real 2Wiki target.
- Runner integration and poison-text review remain pending.

## Isolated v3 development construction gate
- Added `twowiki_v3_development_gate.py`.
- Requires explicit development scope and query membership.
- Requires source-bound, two-AI-screened targets.
- Validates document provenance and supported relation grammar.
- Refuses missing documents and normalized-text duplicates.
- All screened documents remain unapproved for injection.
- This adapter does not invoke a model or change the frozen Week 9 runner.
- Runner registration, new grammar and real review provenance remain pending.

## V3 versioned generation grammar
- Added an explicit development-only grammar profile for film-director
  birth-date questions.
- Three distinct sentence structures are deterministically assigned
  to document slots 0, 1 and 2.
- The validator checks the exact relation, subject, answer and slot.
- No model inference was performed during these synthetic tests.
- Different sentence structures are NOT independent factual sources.
- This grammar covers one question family, not all 2Wiki relations.
- Legacy v2 validation remains the default in the isolated adapter.
- V3 is not registered in the frozen Week 9 runner.
- Real AI-review provenance and poison-text adjudication remain pending.
